Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Clip Rush ("the app", "we") handles and why. The app is a desktop tool that runs on its operator's own computer. It is used to make highlight clips from public live streams and upload them to the operator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Information we handle

  • TikTok account access. When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token, a refresh token and the account's TikTok user ID (open_id). These are used only to upload videos to that account's TikTok drafts.
  • YouTube account access. When the account owner signs in with Google, the app receives tokens that let it upload videos to that owner's YouTube channel.
  • Public stream content. The app records short portions of public Twitch live streams, reads public Twitch chat messages to detect highlight moments, and transcribes clip audio to create captions. Chat messages are counted, not stored.

How information is used

  • To upload finished clips to the signed-in account's TikTok drafts and YouTube channel.
  • To credit the streamer shown in each clip.

We do not use TikTok or Google data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than uploading the account owner's videos.

Where information is stored

Tokens and clip files are stored only on the operator's own computer. We do not run a server that collects or stores user data. Information is sent only to TikTok, Google/YouTube and Twitch, through their official APIs, to perform the actions described above.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. TikTok, Google and Twitch process data under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

  • Recorded stream footage is kept in a rolling buffer of a few minutes and deleted automatically.
  • Tokens are kept until the account owner revokes access or deletes them.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your TikTok account at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Streamers and viewers

If you appear in a clip and want it removed, email aidinc@subclearance.com. We will remove it from our accounts promptly.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
