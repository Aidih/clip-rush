Gaming highlights, minutes after they happen.

Clip Rush is a desktop tool behind a short-form gaming highlights channel. It watches live Twitch gameplay streams, spots the big moments from chat reactions, and turns each one into a vertical clip with captions and an on-screen credit to the streamer.

How it works

  1. The tool follows popular live gameplay streams on Twitch.
  2. When chat erupts, it saves the last half-minute of the stream around that moment.
  3. It edits the moment into a 9:16 clip, adds captions, and credits the streamer by name on screen and in the caption.
  4. The clip is uploaded to the channel owner's TikTok drafts. Nothing is published automatically. The owner reviews each draft in TikTok and decides whether to post it.

For streamers

Every clip credits you by name and links back to your channel. If you'd like a clip removed, or want to talk about clipping for you officially, email aidinc@subclearance.com and we'll respond quickly.
